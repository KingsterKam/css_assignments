Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

Chocolate chip cookies are loved around the world, but not all of them taste the best. Below is the recipe for the best quick and easy chocolate chip cookies out there

Ingredients

Dry Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Wet Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups unsalted butter
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

Add-ins

  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat the Oven to 375°F.
  2. In a bowl, cream together the butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar for about 3-5 minutes. Beat in eggs and vanilla extract.
  3. Add only 2 ½ of the flour to the bowl as well as the baking soda and salt. Mix together.
  4. Add the rest of the flour if the dough is too sticky. The dough should be soft but still hold its shape.
  5. Stir in the chocolate chips.
  6. Drop rounded balls od dough onto the cookie sheet, about 2-3 inches apart.
  7. Bake for 8-10 minutes, or until tops are barely brown.
  8. Remove cookies from the oven and let them rest for 5 minutes before transferring to a cooling rack.

Tips and Tricks

  • Make sure butter is soft, not melted.
  • Use room temperature eggs.
  • Sift the flour.
  • Don't add all the flour in at once.
